# ⚖️ NyayaSetu AI (న్యాయసేతు AI)

> **AI-Powered Legal Aid & Citizen Justice Platform for Indian Citizens**

---

## 🌟 Overview
**NyayaSetu AI** is a citizen justice assistant designed to simplify Indian law for everyday people. It helps citizens understand their legal rights, maps grievances to the new **Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita (BNS 2023)**, drafts court-ready legal notices and police complaints, analyzes agreements for unfair clauses, and provides spoken legal guidance across 8 Indian languages.

---

## 🏛️ Key Capabilities
* **Legal Grievance Triage:** Maps plain-language problems (Cyber Scams, Rental Disputes, Unpaid Salaries, Criminal Harassment) to applicable sections under **BNS 2023** and **BNSS 2023**.
* **1-Click Document Drafter:** Generates formal Pre-Litigation Legal Notices, Police Complaint/FIR applications (under Section 173 BNSS), and Consumer Forum petitions.
* **Contract Simplifier:** Identifies unfair or void terms (such as illegal post-employment non-compete clauses under Section 27 of the Indian Contract Act).
* **Citizen Rights Guide:** Offers quick reference for constitutional protections, arrest safeguards, and free legal aid access (NALSA Helpline: 15100).
* **Multilingual Voice Guidance:** Spoken legal advisory briefings across 8 Indian languages (Telugu, Hindi, English, Tamil, Marathi, Kannada, Odia, Assamese).
